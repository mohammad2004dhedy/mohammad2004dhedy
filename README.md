## Featured Projects

### LawMind — AI-Powered Legal Research Platform
Graduation project (team of 4, I was team lead), successfully defended in June 2026. It helps Palestinian lawyers find relevant legal articles and Court of Cassation decisions from natural-language questions, then generates AI-assisted legal analysis and pleading drafts.

- Retrieval pipeline: multilingual E5 embeddings in Qdrant, semantic search with keyword score boosting, query optimization and relevance filtering with LLMs (Groq)
- JWT/OTP authentication and role-based administration
- Result quality iterated with feedback from a practicing lawyer

`React` `Tailwind CSS` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Qdrant` `LLMs`

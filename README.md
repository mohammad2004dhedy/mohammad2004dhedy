## Featured Project

### LawMind

**AI-Powered Palestinian Legal Research Platform**

LawMind was developed as my graduation project at the Arab American University. It helps Palestinian lawyers search for relevant legal articles and Court of Cassation decisions using hybrid semantic and keyword search.

The platform also provides AI-assisted legal analysis and pleading-draft generation based on retrieved legal content.

#### Key Features

- Semantic and keyword-based legal search
- Retrieval of relevant Palestinian legal articles
- Search across Court of Cassation decisions
- AI-generated legal analysis
- AI-assisted pleading drafts
- Lawyer and administrator roles
- Search history and administrative dashboards
- Secure authentication and role-based access

#### Technologies

`React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `FastAPI` `Python`  
`SQLAlchemy` `PostgreSQL` `Supabase` `Qdrant`  
`JWT Authentication` `LLM Integration` `Semantic Search`
